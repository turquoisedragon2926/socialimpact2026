Where did this come from?

Downloaded snapshots of DataSF neighborhoods, Recreation & Parks properties, library facilities, and USGS 3DEP elevation.

Park data covers RPD properties, not every green space. Library facilities include support and leased sites, not just public branches. Analysis neighborhoods are reporting areas, not legal boundaries.

Additional boundaries: 2020 Census blocks, active map lots, zoning districts, and SFPUC urban watersheds. Parcels load locally as you zoom and pan. Watersheds use the Major_Drainage layer from SFPUC’s map.

From maps to planning

Terrain could inform slope analysis. Parks describe one kind of land use. Facilities locate community resources. Combining them makes questions possible; it does not yet predict flooding or measure access.
