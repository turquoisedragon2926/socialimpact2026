    What the checks establish

    Model and data checks

    • Eight scenarios, evaluated at every year from 2021 through 2061.
    • All seven brushes, both packaged park baselines (2020/2021), five climate settings and six assessment times: 420 cases.
    • Carbon checked against the independent pinned Trends.Earth coefficient fixture.
    • Hectares reconcile; missing inputs remain unknown; the tested scenarios do not change colors in unedited cells.
    • Restoration, clearing, delayed recovery, cover-only decline, and unsupported water interpretations are explicitly checked.

    Browser flows

      Limits that affect the story

      • The vegetation response times are prototype assumptions. We are verifying consistent consequences of those assumptions, not validating them as causal predictions.
      • Only about 7% of this city footprint has soil-carbon estimates. Planting can improve modeled vegetation while the combined map remains unknown.
      • The first ten simulated samples form the productivity reference. Early interventions influence it. Performance uses initial land-cover groups without soil taxonomy.
      • Open-water histories are outside this terrestrial productivity model. Water also does not imply flood protection or water supply.
      • The generic land-cover matrix may call forest-to-grass conversion declining even with unchanged soil carbon. That is a rule, not a locally validated ecological judgement.
      • 2020 and 2021 WorldCover products use different algorithms. Cross-year differences are not clean evidence of real historical change.
      • Declining hectares are a conservative lower bound where source-mask overlap is unknown. A colored cell can be only partly assessed.
